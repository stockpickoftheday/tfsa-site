How to Open a TFSA

Opening a TFSA is about as easy as opening a chequing account. If you can open a bank account, you can open a TFSA. It takes about 15 minutes, and you only need two things.

What you need

  • Your Social Insurance Number (SIN). The provider needs it to report your TFSA to the CRA.
  • ID. Usually a driver’s licence or passport. Some providers ask for two pieces.

You also need to be 18 or older and a resident of Canada for tax purposes. That is it.

How to open a TFSA, step by step

  1. Decide what you want to hold. Cash and GICs? Open a TFSA at your bank. Stocks and ETFs? Open a self-directed TFSA at a brokerage.
  2. Pick a provider. Your bank, a credit union, or an online brokerage. Compare fees: many brokerages charge no fee to hold a TFSA, while some banks charge a yearly fee.
  3. Apply. Online, in a branch, or on the phone. You will give your name, address, date of birth, SIN and ID.
  4. Put money in. Transfer money from your chequing account. Start with whatever you have — there is no minimum at most providers.
  5. Choose what to buy. If it is a self-directed TFSA, buy your GIC, ETF or stock. If it is a bank TFSA, the money sits in cash or a GIC you pick.

That is the whole process. There is no application fee, no waiting period and no test. Your TFSA is open the same day at most providers.

Before you put money in

  • Check your room first. Use the calculator to find your maximum room, or check CRA My Account for your exact room. Putting in more than your room costs 1% per month on the excess.
  • Start small if you are unsure. You can add more later. Unused room never expires.
  • Do not rush the investments. The money can sit in cash while you decide what to buy. The tax-free part starts the day the money is in the account.

Common questions

How do I open a TFSA?

Pick a provider — your bank for cash and GICs, or a brokerage for stocks and ETFs — then apply online, in a branch or by phone with your SIN and ID. It takes about 15 minutes and the account is usually open the same day.

How much money do I need to open a TFSA?

Most providers have no minimum. You can open a TFSA with whatever you have and add more later. Unused contribution room never expires.

Do I need a SIN to open a TFSA?

Yes. You need a valid Social Insurance Number, and you must be 18 or older and a resident of Canada for tax purposes.

Can I open a TFSA online?

Yes. Most banks and brokerages let you open a TFSA online in about 15 minutes, with your SIN and ID.
