How Many TFSAs Can You Have?

As many as you like. There is no limit on the number of TFSA accounts you can open. What is limited is the total amount you can put into all of them together.

One room, shared across every account

The Canada Revenue Agency says it plainly: “You can have more than one TFSA at any given time, but the total amount you contribute to your TFSAs cannot be more than your available TFSA contribution room.” Every TFSA you hold draws from the same pool of room. Opening a second TFSA does not give you a second limit.

That is why the CRA tracks your room as one number. In 2024, the average TFSA holder had 1.7 accounts, according to the CRA’s TFSA statistics — so having more than one is normal.

Why would you want more than one?

  • Different jobs for different accounts. One TFSA in cash for an emergency fund, another invested in ETFs for the long term.
  • Different providers. Your bank may have the best savings rate, while a brokerage has the stocks and ETFs you want.
  • A joint goal with a spouse. Each spouse has their own TFSA room, and one spouse can give the other money to contribute to their own TFSA.

What to watch out for

  • The room is shared. If you have $10,000 of room left, you can put $10,000 into one TFSA, or $6,000 in one and $4,000 in another — not $10,000 in each.
  • It is easier to over-contribute by mistake. With two or three accounts, you have to add up what you put into all of them. The CRA charges 1% per month on the excess.
  • Check CRA My Account. It shows your room across all your TFSAs in one place, though it can lag a few months behind what you actually did.
  • Move money by transfer. To move money between your own TFSAs, ask the receiving provider to transfer it directly. If you withdraw and re-deposit, the money only comes back as room the next January 1.

Common questions

How many TFSAs can I have?

As many as you like — there is no limit on the number of TFSA accounts. But all of them share one contribution room, so opening a second TFSA does not give you more room.

Does having two TFSAs double my contribution room?

No. Your contribution room is one number, shared across every TFSA you hold. The CRA charges 1% per month on any amount you contribute above that room, across all your accounts combined.

Why does the average TFSA holder have 1.7 accounts?

Many people keep one TFSA in cash at a bank and another invested at a brokerage, or separate accounts for different goals. The CRA's 2024 statistics show 32,061,460 accounts held by 19,322,830 holders.
