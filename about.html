About TFSA Guide

This site exists for one reason: the government does not promote the TFSA, and most Canadians think it is just a savings account. It is not. A tax free savings account can hold stocks, ETFs, bonds, GICs and mutual funds — and many Canadians do not know that.

What this site does

  • A calculator that works out your total TFSA contribution room from your date of birth.
  • The rules, made easy — who qualifies, the limits, withdrawals and the over-contribution penalty.
  • The facts — every number on this site comes from the Canada Revenue Agency or Statistics Canada, with the source and the year shown.

Nothing on this site is financial advice. It is general information to help you understand the account, so you can talk to a licensed advisor with better questions.
